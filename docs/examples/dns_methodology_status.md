# Final automatic-DNS methodology status audit

The automatic-DNS methodological development programme is closed. This audit reviews existing reports only. It creates no trajectory, changes no code or threshold, and performs no LISA or termination evaluation. It authorizes no experiment, continuation, deployment, or production use. Historical decisions remain unchanged; earlier reports' invitations to proceed describe their historical disposition and are not current authorization.

The four statuses used here are **VALIDATED**, **DEVELOPMENT-SUPPORTED**, **FAILED / NOT VALIDATED**, and **NOT TESTED**. VALIDATED means that the named component passed its declared mathematical, numerical, or empirical checks within the stated scope. It does not mean universal convergence or production readiness. DEVELOPMENT-SUPPORTED means that useful derivation or development evidence exists without adequate independent validation of the broader claim. FAILED / NOT VALIDATED records a failed relevant validation or an unresolved validation requirement. NOT TESTED identifies a use or integration for which this programme supplies no applicable test.

## Decisive evidence and its scope

**Stage 6B: PASS.** Both LISA pooled-promotion banks completed their declared 384-sweep fixed-contour benchmark and passed structural, numerical, bank-separation and replay checks. This remains a valid result for the frozen population kernel and promotion operation. It established qualitatively compatible populations under its stated criteria, while recording residual calibration log-likelihood drift; it did not establish full physical convergence. The gb_0004 ladder remained frozen through level 12, and the validation contour was not accepted as level 13.

**Stage 7A: PASS within its intended integration scope.** The automatic fixed-contour builder passed candidate/calibration bookkeeping, strict support, fail-without-retry behavior, promotion and checkpoint checks. A fresh interpreter resumed a completed checkpoint and reproduced the uninterrupted result exactly. These are valid engineering results. Later failures limit the statistical interpretation of automatic construction, not the historical bookkeeping or restart pass.

**Stage 7B: PASS within its estimator-validation scope.** Fresh fixed-contour reconstruction strata passed analytic Gaussian and asymmetric-mixture evidence, posterior, CDF and modal-probability checks at the frozen tolerances. Exact saved reconstruction and restart comparisons passed. The MIS estimator uses every eligible stratum in its mixture denominator and includes the prior stratum. It does not silently pool adaptive construction samples.

**Stage 8: FAIL under the original criterion.** The validation incorrectly required the terminal remainder bound to cover total evidence-estimator error. Such a bound addresses a terminal target integral, not all mass-calibration, finite-sampling or estimator error. The failure remains the official decision; correcting the interpretation does not retroactively produce a pass.

**Stage 8R: corrected semantics derived, termination not validated.** The audit separated unresolved terminal evidence from total reconstruction error and derived the appropriate normalization against already resolved evidence. A global likelihood upper bound must be justified; an observed maximum is insufficient. Stage-7B full-support MIS already accounts for the terminal contour, so terminal smallness is not a general bound on its Monte Carlo error.

**Stage 8H: held-out FAIL before termination.** The automatic selection ESS gate failed while proposing level 4, after three accepted levels. The stopping criterion never triggered. This establishes a construction-information failure, not a successful test of corrected termination. The failed selection history was not serialized, limiting finer retrospective diagnosis.

**Stage 9A: CASE C.** Increasing the fixed-contour mixing budget to 1x, 2x and 4x did not restore deep mode switching: both banks had zero physical switches at construction levels 4–6 at every budget. All tested budgets passed their compression ESS gates. No robust production budget was established.

**Stage 9B: architectural finding.** The audited automatic construction used a scalar fixed generating contour and particle states without per-walker assigned indices. It omitted genuine DNS within-run level backtracking. Raising a contour between accepted construction attempts is not level diffusion during sampling. This finding does not invalidate the original augmented DNS target or frozen index kernel.

**Stage 9C: development CASE A.** Genuine level diffusion restored deep cross-mode connectivity on a development toy. Selection/calibration top-switch totals at deep generating indices 3, 4 and 5 were 384/351, 274/275 and 257/258, compared with zero in matched fixed-contour controls. The declared development conditional/compression checks and exact restart comparison passed. This supported a held-out test at that time; it did not itself supply independent production validation.

**Stage 9D: independent FAILED-CONDITIONAL.** The three-mode held-out calibration trace failed the declared conditional CDF, moment and modal-weight checks at candidate 5, despite compression ESS approximately 13,329. Four candidates had been accepted; the fifth was not appended and the sixth was not attempted. Its held-out restart was never launched. The later saved-data studies made this model development data; none changes the failed decision.

**Stage 9D-F: causal diagnosis inconclusive.** Saved-record checks found no detailed-balance, stale-threshold or deterministic collection defect sufficient to explain the failure. All modes were reachable and actual excursions occurred. No incorrect stationary joint target was established. First-visit initialization, incomplete finite-time equilibration and a rare correlated realization remained unresolved alternatives; reachability did not establish the realized modal weights.

**Stage 9E: CASE F — STILL INCONCLUSIVE.** The historical failed trace approached the correct conditional distribution in later saved windows. The bounded paired promoted-start/exact-joint-stationary control study did not resolve a reproducible early bias, a collector distortion, or the probability of the historical discrepancy. Later observations do not replace the officially failed retained sample. Exact stationary physical initialization also does not automatically certify the first future top visit's trace-chain initialization.

**Stage 9F: CASE B — TOO MANY FALSE REJECTIONS.** The frozen generic rank-R-hat/bulk-tail-ESS composite detected the historical failure but rejected all eight exact-joint-stationary controls, all eight promoted controls and the successful Stage-9D selection sample. Theta projection rank R-hat and bulk ESS triggered every rejection; tail ESS did not. Thus the diagnostics exposed slow conditional information without useful historical-failure-versus-control discrimination. The rule did not require analytic mode labels, but was not validated as a generic high-dimensional gate. No thresholds were retuned and no final held-out validation was justified.

## Three separate requirements

A reliable automatic construction needs **compression reliability**, **cross-mode connectivity**, and **conditional-distribution stationarity** together.

Compression reliability concerns estimating the strict-tail ratio X_(J+1)/X_J with adequate observable-specific information and credible uncertainty. The compression ESS ≥20 check remains an approximate diagnostic for that indicator. High compression ESS does not establish cross-mode mixing: an indicator can fluctuate rapidly inside each mode while mode occupancy changes slowly.

Cross-mode connectivity concerns routes and actual transitions between relevant regions, including descents to connected lower contours and returns. Cross-mode round trips do not establish correct finite-time modal weights: a chain can visit every mode while its retained residence fractions remain wrong. Reachability, witnessed transitions and balanced finite-window occupation are different claims.

Conditional-distribution stationarity concerns whether the retained assigned-top samples adequately represent pi(theta) I[A_J]/X_J during level construction. The exact conditional invariant-measure identity does not prove that promoted, burned-in, first-K retained samples already have that law. Physical time and censored top-visit order offer distinct diagnostic views. Compression and connectivity successes cannot compensate for a failed conditional-distribution check.

## What remains usable

**A. Validated numerical building blocks.** The original frozen joint target and index transition, the specified constrained-prior slice transition, bank-local pooled promotion, CPU/x64 cache/copy contract, fixed-contour LISA population operation, Stage-7A orchestration bookkeeping, Stage-7B estimator checks, and the separately scoped restart guarantees retain their established results. Each is usable as a reference building block under its original assumptions. Their status does not authorize an execution or certify the statistical validity of an assembled automatic pipeline.

**B. Useful research/development components.** Adaptive construction primitives, Stage-9C connectivity evidence, corrected terminal-remainder semantics and descriptive conditional-observable diagnostics remain useful for understanding the methodology. The failed held-out trace and all Stage-9E/9F controls are development evidence. They cannot serve as new independent validation or justify post-hoc threshold selection.

**C. Components that must not be described as production-ready.** Statistically reliable automatic fixed-contour construction, the automatic diffusive ladder, the conditional-stationarity acceptance gate, automatic termination, and end-to-end automatic LISA inference/stopping lack the necessary validation. No combination of the scoped passes supplies the missing finite-time stationarity result.

## LISA consequence

The previous gb_0004 CPU/x64 numerical and Stage-6B fixed-contour population/promotion results remain valid independently of later automatic-ladder failures. The population kernel used frozen labelled slice proposals and reciprocal source exchanges with joint prior and reverse/forward selector corrections, under a common fixed contour. Later scalar-toy failures neither refute those checks nor justify expanding their scope.

The original limitations remain: residual finite-window drift, interacting walkers, model/configuration specificity and no complete posterior/evidence convergence claim. Stage 5B remains failed; Stage 6B did not append level 13. A common-contour exchange validation does not validate exchanges among diffusive walkers at different assigned thresholds. The automatic diffusive ladder is not production-ready for LISA, and its LISA population integration is NOT TESTED. No LISA calculation was performed for this audit.

## Reconstruction consequence

Stage-7B reconstruction remains independently VALIDATED within its analytic toy and record/replay scope. It freezes the ladder, then uses fresh separately seeded fixed-contour reconstruction streams. Construction-time diffusive samples are not inputs to its authoritative estimator, so Stage-9D's construction failure does not invalidate the MIS derivation or the recorded Stage-7B accuracy results.

This independence is methodological, not immunity to bad inputs. Reconstruction still requires adequate conditional sampling in its own streams, positive supporting allocations, the prior stratum, correct caches and meaningful calibrated masses. Estimated masses produce a plug-in estimator; exact unbiasedness is not claimed under mass error, and normalized posterior ratios have finite-sample bias. Stage-7B validation does not certify reconstruction on an unreliable newly constructed ladder or establish LISA inference accuracy.

## Termination and restart consequences

Corrected termination semantics exist, but automatic termination remains unvalidated. No reliable held-out automatic ladder has yet provided the end-to-end setting needed to validate it. A terminal-remainder criterion cannot absorb unrelated calibration, sampling or estimator error. Neither Stage-8R's derivation nor isolated historical stability/restart checks convert Stage 8 or Stage 8H into a pass.

Restartability has three distinct validated scopes: Stage-7A fixed automatic construction at completed-iteration boundaries; Stage-7B reconstruction archive/input/output reproducibility with its recorded fresh-process construction continuation; and Stage-9C diffusive development continuation including a saved within-attempt collection snapshot. Stage-7B reused its existing reconstruction archives in the fresh-process comparison, rather than generating new reconstruction trajectories in that branch. Stage-9D's held-out restart was not reached. Successful restart tests show reproducible state and computation; they do not imply stationary samples, correct modal weights, accurate calibration or valid termination.

## Final scientific conclusion

Automatic DNS ladder construction is not production-validated.

Further progress requires a new methodological idea for certifying
finite-time conditional stationarity, not additional post-hoc
threshold tuning on the existing development traces.
